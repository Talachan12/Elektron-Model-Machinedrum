Machinedrum machines for the Model:Cycles

Three Machinedrum SPS-1 UW machines, ported sample for sample from its DSP, as machines 7, 8 and 9 of the Model:Cycles (OS 1.13). The firmware is built here, in your browser, from your own official files. Nothing is uploaded.

TrxB2TRX-B2 kick
TrxSDTRX-SD snare
TrxCHTRX-CH closed hi-hat

3. Build

Download model-cycles_OS1.13_md.syx

4. Flash

  1. Back up your projects with Elektron Transfer.
  2. Connect a MIDI interface to the Cycles' MIDI IN (TRS jack): it is also your way back to the official OS.
  3. Hold FUNC while switching on, choose OS UPGRADE (TRIG 4), then send the .syx with any SysEx tool. Details: FLASH.md.
  4. To go back, flash the official model-cycles_OS1.13.syx the same way.

A modified firmware is flashed at your own risk and may affect your warranty. Not affiliated with Elektron.
