Patch your Model:Cycles in three steps

You need two official files from elektron.se. They stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded.

Add both files above to enable the button.

⬇ Download model-cycles_OS1.13_md.syx
Then flash it
  1. Back up your projects with Elektron Transfer.
  2. Connect a MIDI interface to the Cycles' MIDI IN (TRS jack): it is also your way back to the official OS.
  3. Hold FUNC while switching on, choose OS UPGRADE (TRIG 4), then send the .syx with any SysEx tool. Details: FLASH.md.
  4. To go back, flash the official model-cycles_OS1.13.syx the same way.

A modified firmware is flashed at your own risk and may affect your warranty. Not affiliated with Elektron.

Pick a track, open its machine setting and turn LEVEL/DATA past CHORD: TrxB2, TrxSD and TrxCH are machines 7, 8 and 9, usable on any of the six tracks. PUNCH off keeps the Machinedrum defaults. The TrxSD attack sample and the TrxSD and TrxCH filter tables are read from your Machinedrum OS. TrxCH SWEEP is an extra: the resonance of its filters, at 70 exactly the Machinedrum. Not combinable with the Modded-Cycles Syntakt engines, MACRO or Model-TG.
